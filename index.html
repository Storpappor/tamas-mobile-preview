Keyboard

  • ← → ↑ ↓ or WASD move
  • C duck
  • Space jump
  • J punch   K kick   L block
  • J+K together: grab and throw
  • Double-tap a direction to sprint
  • Hold away from a strike to block it
  • I Ajsich (kiai): double damage for 4 s
  • P or Esc pause

Tips

  • Duck under high kicks, jump over low sweeps.
  • Hold block, or hold away from a strike, to absorb it.
  • A throw goes through a guard; strikes do not.
  • Ajsich breaks guards but needs time to recharge. Caught mid-kick? Dra tillbaka benet.
  • Land hits without getting hit to build a combo.
  • Step up or down to line up a strike, or to slip out of one.
  • Clear each fight, then follow the GO arrow.
  • Block, duck or sidestep throwing stars.
